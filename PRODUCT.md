# PRODUCT

## Register
product

## Product purpose
ETF Optimizer is a local research dashboard and CLI toolkit for building ETF universes, downloading public market data, running multicriteria ELECTRE selection, comparing walk-forward portfolio benchmarks, and producing reproducible methodology artifacts for a thesis-grade ETF optimization workflow.

## Primary users
- Quantitative researcher validating ETF universe and backtest methodology.
- Thesis author preparing reproducible evidence and audit trails.
- Power user who prefers transparent local execution over a hosted SaaS workflow.

## Jobs to be done
- Run the available data and experiment scripts without memorizing long command lines.
- Inspect generated results, benchmark comparisons, eligibility funnels, methodology notes, and manifests.
- Understand whether a run is methodologically comparable, reproducible, and ready for review.

## Tone
Precise, calm, academic, and operational. The product should feel like a native macOS research instrument rather than a marketing page.

## Anti-references
- No finance cliché palette of navy and gold.
- No trading terminal maximalism.
- No ornamental gradients or decorative glass panels.
- No claims that yfinance or active-current universes are survivorship-bias-free.
